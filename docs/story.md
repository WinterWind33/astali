# The story of Astali

## The name

**Astali** stands for **A ST**udent **A**ll **LI**fe.

To me, being a student isn't only something you are for a few years at school. Anyone who keeps learning, planning
and building is one, for life. I made Astali for them.

## Why I made it

There are good tools for planning, studying and organizing work, but more and more of them come with a price tag.
Tools I relied on were free for years, until they became paid products too. When useful tools turn into
subscriptions, the people who lose them first are the ones who can least afford them, and students with low incomes
are usually among them.

I wanted every student to have a tool like this for free: to plan, to study, to keep track of their projects, and to
have fun doing it. Students are the people who will shape the future, and they shouldn't need money to get organized.

## How it started

In September 2022 I started `astali-dart`, a small Flutter app I described as a "WIP App for students". It was a
bulletin board of post-its, nothing more.

Astali grew from there one feature at a time, mostly because I use it every day and kept noticing what was missing.
Kanban boards with columns and tasks came next, then tags, GitHub issue import, activity history with undo, a decision
log, plan boards for implementation plans, and an MCP server so AI agents can work in the same vault. In 1.5.0 the
post-it board came back as **Notes boards**, a feature of the app Astali started as.

I've built Astali in close collaboration with Claude, as my AI pair programmer.

## My promise

Astali is, and always will be:

- **Free.** No price, no subscription, no "pro" tier, no features locked behind a paywall.
- **Open source.** The code is under the [MIT License](../LICENSE). Read it, change it, share it.
- **Ad-free.** No ads, no tracking, no selling your data.

If Astali helps you study, plan or build something, that's all I made it for.
